# Steam Hours

A Windows desktop app for managing a local Steam session, choosing games and checking the playtime reported by Steam.

## Download

### [View Windows releases](https://github.com/aksoysavas4422-hue/steam-saat-releases/releases)

**Download `SteamHours.exe` and open it.** The first launch prepares the app in the background and opens the dashboard. Later launches open the app directly. You do not need Node.js, a terminal or a setup wizard.

The other release files support background updates. You only need **SteamHours.exe**.

## What it does

- Start and stop a local Steam game session with the games you select.
- Search the Steam Store, save favorites and see Steam-reported playtime separately from the local session timer.
- Use Squad and Counter-Strike 2 as quick picks. Open FiveM through a local shortcut; FiveM does not accumulate Steam playtime through this app.
- Keep the app in the system tray, reconnect after connection loss and optionally start selected games when you sign in to Windows.
- Download updates in the background and apply them when you quit the app.

## Choose your language

English is the default. Click a flag at the **top right** to switch to **English, Türkçe, Русский or 中文**. The change applies immediately and is remembered for the next launch; no language pack download is needed.

The app runs on your PC. It cannot keep a session running while the PC is asleep or switched off. It stops when another Steam game session takes priority.

Steam playtime is not guaranteed. Automation may expose your account to restrictions. The application is unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown publisher notice.
